The roll, so far

How it started — and who helped it grow

I first got my camera from my elder brother. I didn't use it on a daily basis at first, but things changed when I brought it to my freshers party and started taking photos of my friends. That was the first time I actually felt the weight of capturing a real moment.

After that, we started taking it everywhere — the zoo, Raj Bhavan, and of course, all around the university campus itself. I don't remember all the specifics now, but the images in this portfolio are a random collection of those times. I'm not a veteran photographer; some of these shots are bad, and some are good. But they are all real, and they all happened.
